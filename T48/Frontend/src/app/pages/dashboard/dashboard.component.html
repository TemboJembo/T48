Recent Innovations in Clean Energy

The world of clean energy continues to expand as we march into the future. An ever more important field as we continue to use nonrenewable energy sources. Solar power, for example, is always at the forefront of the discussion when this topic is broached. Recent innovations have seen the efficiency of solar farms increased as solar cells have become more advanced and efficient in regard to absorbing the sun's energy. This creates a plethora of new opportunities in just about any field as solar power becomes a more viable alternative to traditional power sources. In particular, photovoltaic materials are becoming more advanced. This is leading to not only more efficient solar devices, but also increasing their accessibility. This comes by way of solar devices using this technology being more affordable and easy to deploy in environments where solar may have otherwise been incapable of performing to a high standard before. Not only is the energy harvesting itself becoming more efficient, but so too are the storage methods for energy becoming more robust. A large drawback of something like solar energy is having to consider periods without sunlight, providing little to no power. Due to advancements in energy storage, creating a power bank to overcome these periods of fallow is very much a reality.
